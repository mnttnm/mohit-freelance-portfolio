How they compare

  • Concept A (Gallery Lead) — strongest “show the work” hit: big screenshots dominate right after a tiny hero. Best if the screenshots are your most convincing asset. Slightly more scrolling on mobile before the pitch lands.
  • Concept B (Hero Showcase Split) — proof and pitch share the first screen, so a desktop visitor sees a real product without scrolling at all. Tighter overall; the 2×2 grid carries proof tags (“8K users”, etc.). Mobile stacks the featured shot directly under the headline.
  • Both cut “Who I work with” and “How I work,” compress services to cards, and pull testimonials + screenshots up — roughly halving the text you scroll through on mobile.
